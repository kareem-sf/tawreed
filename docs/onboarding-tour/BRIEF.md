## Intent

Show the real Tawreed workflow as a calm desktop walkthrough for construction engineers:
choose a language, add a BOQ, choose online or offline processing, review package counts and
percentages, and create the workbooks. The visual language is minimal white/gray with amber
only for actions and attention.

## Customizations

- Separate English and Modern Standard Arabic narration and captions.
- Male tutorial voices.
- Fully local MP4, VTT, and poster assets bundled with the desktop app.

## Notes

- No AI jargon, model badges, confidence scores, or decorative construction imagery.
- The original BOQ remains unchanged and offline processing stays available.
