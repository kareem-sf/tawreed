## Frame 1 — Language

Choose English or Arabic before any other setup.

## Frame 2 — Add BOQ

Drop an Excel, PDF, CSV, or ODS bill of quantities. Tawreed reads it locally.

## Frame 3 — Online or offline

Explain the exact extracted fields shared with a connected service and keep offline as an
equally visible choice.

## Frame 4 — Clear progress

Show reading, preparing packages, and creating files with a cancel-safe loader.

## Frame 5 — Review packages

Each package shows its item count, percentage of the full BOQ, and total value. Flag only
items that need a quick check.

## Frame 6 — Create and open

Approve the grouping, create the master and package workbooks, and open the result.

## Frame 7 — Settings

Language, appearance, processing choice, connection, and replayable guidance remain in
Settings.
