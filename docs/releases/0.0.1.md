The first public release of Tawreed: drop your bills of quantities, and Tawreed turns them into procurement
packages, with a master workbook and one workbook per package.

## Download

- **Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit):** [Tawreed_x64-setup.exe](https://github.com/qs-mind/tawreed/releases/latest/download/Tawreed_x64-setup.exe)
- **Linux (64-bit):** [Tawreed_amd64.AppImage](https://github.com/qs-mind/tawreed/releases/latest/download/Tawreed_amd64.AppImage)
  (make it executable, then open it)

The Windows installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Choose
**More info**, then **Run anyway**. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists each file's checksum.

## What's in it

- Reads Excel, CSV, ODS, PDF and scanned pages, in English, Arabic or both, with no template to follow.
- Proposes the packages, places every item in exactly one package, and shows you every item it is unsure of.
- You approve at four points: overlapping files, the package plan, uncertain items and the final workbooks.
- Codes, descriptions, units, quantities and rates reach the workbooks exactly as in your BOQ, and every number
  links back to its cell or page.
- Works with your own AI key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI or an OpenAI-compatible service) or a ChatGPT
  subscription through the official Codex app. A project's content goes to that service only after you agree.
- Your files and workbooks stay on your PC, in `~/.tawreed`.
- English and Arabic, right to left.
- Updates itself: when a new version is out, Tawreed offers it, and installs it only when you press
  **Update and restart**.

More at [qsmind.app/en/tawreed](https://qsmind.app/en/tawreed).
