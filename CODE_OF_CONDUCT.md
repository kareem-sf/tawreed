# Code of conduct

Contributors communicate professionally, review work on its technical merit, and avoid harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, and disclosure of private or customer information.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may restrict participation when conduct threatens a safe,
productive project. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, [@kareem-sf](https://github.com/kareem-sf),
or with GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
option. Reports are handled in confidence.
